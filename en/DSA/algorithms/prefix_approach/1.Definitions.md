# Prefix and suffix

## Definitions

**Prefix** of the sequence (array / string / etc.) is any amount of the first elements (even zero).

**Suffix** - similar to prefix, but the last elements.

### Amount of prefixes and suffixes

For array of size $N$ there are $N + 1$ prefixes and $N + 1$ suffixes (including empty ones).

In the most cases it's convenient to numerate prefixes and suffixes by the amount of elements they contain:

- $Pref_0 = [\ ]$;
- $Pref_1 = [A_1]$;
- $Pref_2 = [A_1, A_2]$;
- $\ldots$
- $Pref_N = [A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_N]$.

From the indexation point of view it's easier to use semi-open intervals: $(0, i]$ for prefixes and $[i, N)$ for suffixes.
